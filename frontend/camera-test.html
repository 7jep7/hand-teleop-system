Troubleshooting Camera Issues:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Site Settings → Camera
  • Safari: Safari → Preferences → Websites → Camera
  • System: System Preferences → Security & Privacy → Privacy → Camera
  • HTTPS Required: Some browsers require HTTPS for camera access (localhost usually works)
